Web-based Price Reporting System

Automating market trend analysis for the DTI-IX Consumer Protection Division.

PHP MySQL Bootstrap 5 3NF Database Design

Project Overview

The Consumer Protection Division handles extensive daily monitoring logs to track market trends and pricing. This project was developed to transition the team from manual data handling to an automated, centralized web system. The primary goal was to allow staff to upload securely and instantly calculate price ranges and overarching market trends.

Database Architecture & ERD

A significant portion of the planning phase involved designing a robust database architecture. To prevent data anomalies and ensure fast query performance when calculating price ranges, the database was rigorously modeled.

  • Normalization: The schema was normalized to the 3rd Normal Form (3NF) to eliminate redundant data regarding product categories and monitoring locations.
  • Relationships: Designed clear Entity-Relationship Diagrams (ERD) mapping the daily monitoring reports to specific products, vendors, and authorized staff members.
